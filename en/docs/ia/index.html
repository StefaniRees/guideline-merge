Core Principle — C6: AI as cross-cutting support

AI is positioned as a contextual support mechanism — not a replacement — for human decisions. In all uses, empirical evidence reinforces the need for continuous human supervision, especially to mitigate risks related to the introduction of vulnerabilities and misguided technical decisions.
