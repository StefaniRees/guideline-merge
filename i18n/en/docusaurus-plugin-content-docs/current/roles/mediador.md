## Description
Neutral role responsible for **supervising the merge flow**, facilitating resolutions, and ensuring that Quality Gates are followed.

Corresponds to the **OpenUP Integrator** role — responsible for artifact integration, flow coordination, and risk mitigation. Makes the function of a specialized integrator explicit, ensuring that critical decisions are recorded and communicated.

## Activities
- [Code Review](/docs/activities/code-review)
- [Resolve Merge Conflicts](/docs/activities/resolver-conflitos)
- [Final Merge](/docs/activities/merge-final)

## Produced Artifacts
- [Review Checklist](/docs/artifacts/checklist-revisao)
- [Conflict History](/docs/artifacts/historico-conflitos)
- [Decision Record](/docs/artifacts/registro-decisao)

## Responsibilities
- Supervise the merge flow
- Conduct Code Review with a technical checklist
- Formally authorize integration according to team policies
- Support non-trivial conflict resolutions
- Ensure that critical decisions are recorded and communicated
