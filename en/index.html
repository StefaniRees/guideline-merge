Why MergeTrace?

Quality-driven merge

Collaboration

Structured collaboration

Explicit roles (Developer A, Developer B, Mediator) ensure every technical decision is shared, recorded, and traceable.

Learn more →
Quality

Quality-driven review

Objective checklists and quality gates at each phase eliminate ambiguities and reduce late integration conflicts.

Traceability

End-to-end traceability

Grounded in empirical evidence

Built from rigorous research

MergeTrace combines a systematic literature mapping, a quantitative developer survey, and qualitative interviews with industry specialists — resulting in an empirically grounded and methodologically consistent guideline.
