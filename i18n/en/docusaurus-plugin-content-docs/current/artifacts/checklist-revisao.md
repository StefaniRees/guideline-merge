## Description
Checklist that guides peer review, covering style, correctness, tests, security, and documentation.

## Best Practices
- Ensure minimum checkpoints: **compiles and tests**, **minimum coverage**, **linters/Sonar**, **security (secrets/deps)**, **updated docs**.
- Comments must be **addressed and resolved** before final approval.

## Justification
Standardizes quality inspection, reduces variability and human errors.
